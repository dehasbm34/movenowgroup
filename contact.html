Common Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How quickly will you respond? +
Within 1 hour, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For emergency situations, we aim for immediate response, our emergency line is always staffed by a human, not a bot.
Is it safe to contact you from inside Iran, Sudan, or other monitored countries? +
Yes. We use encrypted channels. We recommend using Signal or WhatsApp with a VPN for maximum security. Do not use your real name in initial messages. Our systems are designed for high-risk communications.
Do you charge for consultations? +
No. Initial consultations are always free. We will assess your situation and provide a clear, upfront quote before any work begins. No hidden fees, no commitments.
What languages do you speak? +
Our team speaks English, Farsi, Arabic, Turkish, Russian, French, and more. We have interpretation services available for all major world languages, including Pashto, Dari, Burmese, Ukrainian, Spanish, and others.
Do you have a physical office? +
For security reasons, we do not operate physical client-facing offices. All services are delivered remotely, with field operators deploying as needed. This model protects both our clients and our staff.
