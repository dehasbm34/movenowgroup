About Us

Built for people whose lives cross borders.

Since 2015, Move Now Group has helped families and executives move themselves, their families, and their futures across borders, through private aviation, official citizenship and residency programs, and fully managed relocations.

Who We Are

One firm, three disciplines

Most clients who need a charter flight also need a visa. Most families pursuing a second citizenship eventually need help moving. We built one firm around that reality, so a single team, aviation brokers, mobility advisors, and relocation managers, can carry a client from first consultation to settled arrival.

We work exclusively through licensed and regulated channels: certificated air carriers, government-authorized citizenship agents, and licensed immigration counsel in each destination. It is slower to build a business this way. It is also the only way our clients' outcomes are safe.

Our Standards

What you can hold us to

01

Straight answers first

You get a truthful assessment, including "this isn't possible" or "this will take a year", before any payment is requested. We would rather lose an engagement than start one on a false promise.

02

Discretion as a discipline

We never publicize clients, routes, or engagements. Internally, information is shared only with the people working your file. Externally, we meet every disclosure the law requires, and none it doesn't.

03

Licensed channels only

No shortcuts, no "special arrangements," no gray routes. Every flight, filing, and application runs through the operators, agents, and attorneys authorized to handle it.
