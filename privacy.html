Privacy Policy

How Move Now Group handles enquiry details, service coordination data, and confidential client information.

Legal review note: This page is written for visitor transparency and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before it is treated as final legal advice or a complete regulatory notice.

Who We Are

Move Now Group coordinates private aviation, urgent travel support, documentation readiness, and relocation planning for private clients. Privacy matters because many enquiries involve sensitive travel, family, security, or cross-border information.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to understand an enquiry, assess feasibility, and coordinate the requested service.

  • Contact details such as name, email, phone, WhatsApp, and preferred contact time.
  • Travel details such as origin, destination, timing, passenger count, luggage, pets, and special requirements.
  • Document readiness details when you ask for passport, visa, residency, citizenship, or relocation support.
  • Payment, invoice, compliance, and identity information when required by law, operators, banks, or service partners.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, coordinate lawful routes, communicate with relevant service providers, complete compliance checks, manage invoices, and protect client safety and service quality.

Confidentiality And Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only when it is needed to coordinate the service, comply with law, process payment, confirm aviation or travel logistics, or work with licensed professionals. Examples include aircraft operators, ground handlers, payment processors, attorneys, regulated immigration professionals, and government or carrier systems where required.

Retention And Security

We keep information only as long as needed for service delivery, legal recordkeeping, compliance, dispute handling, and fraud prevention. We use access controls and reasonable technical safeguards, but no email, web form, or messaging system can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

Cookies And Analytics

The website may use basic technical cookies and analytics tools to understand performance and improve the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal, compliance, safety, accounting, and operational limits. To make a privacy request, contact info@movenowgroup.com.
