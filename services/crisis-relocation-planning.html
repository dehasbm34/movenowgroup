Planning Scope

What we prepare before you move

Good relocation planning turns a rushed decision into a controlled process. We focus on legal pathways, practical logistics, and clear next steps.

Step 1

Destination review

We compare realistic destination options based on nationality, current location, family structure, budget, language, schooling, work needs, and route availability.

  • Temporary stay and residence pathways
  • Schooling and family considerations
  • Cost-of-living and accommodation ranges
  • Travel feasibility and airport access
Step 2

Route and travel plan

We map lawful routing options and identify practical travel requirements before bookings or charter options are discussed.

  • Commercial and private travel options
  • Transit country considerations
  • Ground transport and airport transfer planning
  • Contingency routes if schedules change
Step 3

Arrival setup

The move does not end at the airport. We prepare arrival logistics so the first days are organized.

  • Temporary accommodation search
  • Local transport, SIM, banking, and registration checklist
  • School and family support research
  • Introductions to vetted local professionals
Lawful Routes Only

We separate realistic options from risky shortcuts

Relocation decisions can affect travel records, future residency, family safety, and long-term options. We focus on lawful routes and involve licensed professionals where legal advice is required.

  • No irregular border-crossing arrangements
  • No guarantees of visa, residence, asylum, or border outcomes
  • No claims of government authority or special government status
  • Licensed attorneys or regulated advisors used where legal advice is needed

FAQ

Relocation planning questions

What does crisis relocation planning include?

Planning may include lawful destination review, route planning, travel coordination, temporary accommodation, school and housing research, local registration steps, transport, and introductions to licensed legal or immigration professionals where legal advice is needed.

Do you provide legal advice?

Move Now Group is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. When legal advice is required, we coordinate with licensed attorneys or regulated immigration professionals in the relevant jurisdiction.

Can you guarantee a visa, residency, or relocation outcome?

No. No private company can guarantee a government decision, visa, residency status, asylum outcome, or border decision. We help clients prepare information, understand lawful options, and coordinate practical logistics.

Is the planning confidential?

Yes. Relocation planning is handled discreetly and information is shared only with the people required to assess or coordinate the requested service, subject to applicable laws and official requirements.
