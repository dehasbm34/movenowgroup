Common Questions

Charter, answered plainly

How quickly can you arrange a charter?

Usually 2–6 hours from the moment you confirm and payment is arranged. In extreme emergencies, active conflict escalation, medical evacuation, we have arranged departure in under 3 hours where permits allowed. The more notice you can give us, the better your aircraft options and pricing.

Can I fly from a country under sanctions?

In many cases, yes. We operate strictly through legal international aviation frameworks and work with operators licensed in jurisdictions permitted to serve the route. We have extensive experience arranging flights from Iran, Lebanon, Venezuela, and other complex jurisdictions. Each situation is assessed individually against current sanctions and aviation rules, and we tell you honestly what is and isn't possible.

Will my name appear on a passenger manifest?

Manifests and customs filings required by the departure and arrival countries are always completed, that compliance is exactly what keeps your flight lawful and protected. What we control is everything else: private charters do not appear on public booking systems the way commercial tickets do, and we never disclose client identities or itineraries to anyone not legally entitled to them.

Can I bring more than normal luggage?

Yes. Private charters do not have standard airline restrictions, substantial personal effects, artwork, documents, equipment, and valuables can travel with you, and cargo-only charters are available. We handle customs declarations and permit requirements on your behalf.

Do you fly into active conflict zones?

This is assessed case by case. We have operators experienced in high-risk environments with conflict-zone insurance and appropriate security protocols. Flights into or out of active war zones carry additional costs, lead times, and legal review. We will give you an honest assessment of feasibility, we do not make promises we cannot keep.

How do I pay?

By bank wire against an itemized invoice, with full escrow arrangements available for large transactions so your funds are protected until the aircraft is confirmed. As a licensed brokerage we complete standard client identification checks before accepting funds.
