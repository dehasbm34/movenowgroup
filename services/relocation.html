Step by Step

Understanding the asylum process

Every situation is different, but most asylum journeys follow these six stages. We support you at every single step.

1

Safety first

Reach a safe country through a lawful route, or a safe location within your current country. This is the immediate priority, before any paperwork. Contact us at any stage of this step.

2

UNHCR registration

Register as a Person of Concern with UNHCR if you are outside your home country in a transit country. This provides immediate legal protection and begins the documentation process. We accompany you.

3

National asylum application

Submit an asylum application in the destination country where you are present. Deadlines and procedures vary; we ensure you apply correctly, completely, and within required timeframes.

4

Asylum interview

The most critical step, how your case is presented can determine everything. We prepare you thoroughly and arrange legal representation in 20+ countries.

5

Decision & appeal

If initially rejected, do not give up, we support appeals in all countries. Many successful cases are won on appeal with the right legal representation.

6

Status & settlement

Once status is granted, we begin settlement support: housing, employment connections, language programs, school enrollment, banking, and community integration. The journey doesn't end at approval.
