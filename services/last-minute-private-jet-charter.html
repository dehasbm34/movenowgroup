What We Check

Aircraft availability is only the first step

A fast quote still needs to be a usable quote. Before recommending an option, we check the practical details that decide whether the aircraft can actually depart.

  • Nearest suitable aircraft and live repositioning requirements
  • Airport operating hours, FBO access, slots, and ground handling
  • Crew duty time, weather, overflight approvals, and route legality
  • Passenger information, luggage fit, pets, medical needs, or security requirements

FAQ

Last-minute charter questions

How fast can a last-minute private jet depart?

Some routes can depart the same day after aircraft, crew, slot, payment, passenger information, and required handling are confirmed. Timing always depends on operator availability, airport access, weather, permits, and lawful routing.

Do you operate the aircraft?

Move Now Group coordinates charter requests as a broker or coordinator. Flights are operated by certificated third-party air carriers that are responsible for aircraft operations, safety, crew, and regulatory compliance.

What information is needed for a same-day quote?

We need departure airport or city, destination, passenger count, travel date and time, luggage details, pet or medical requirements, and a direct phone or WhatsApp contact.

Can you quote an empty leg for urgent travel?

When a suitable repositioning aircraft is available, we can include an empty-leg option. Empty legs are route-specific and schedule-sensitive, so they are not guaranteed for urgent departures.
