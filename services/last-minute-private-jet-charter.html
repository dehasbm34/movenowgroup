What We Check

Aircraft availability is only the first step

A fast quote still needs to be usable. Before recommending an option, we check the details that decide whether the aircraft can actually depart.

  • +Nearest suitable aircraft and live repositioning requirements
  • +Airport hours, FBO access, slots, and ground handling
  • +Crew duty time, weather, overflight approvals, and route legality
  • +Passenger details, luggage fit, pets, medical needs, or security requirements

Move Now Group does not operate aircraft. Flights are operated by certificated third-party air carriers. Every departure is subject to availability, permits, weather, payment, airport access, and lawful routing.
