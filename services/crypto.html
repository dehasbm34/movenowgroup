Frequently Asked Questions

Is this legal? +
OTC crypto trading is legal in most jurisdictions. We guide clients through relevant compliance requirements for their specific country and transaction type, ensuring full legal compliance at every step.
Do I need to provide ID? +
It depends on the transaction size and method. We aim to minimize documentation requirements while remaining compliant with applicable regulations. Small transactions may require no documentation at all.
What countries do you serve? +
All countries, including those with banking restrictions. We have specific experience serving clients from Iran, Afghanistan, Sudan, Venezuela, Myanmar, Syria, Lebanon, Yemen, Gaza/Palestine, Iraq, Ukraine, Libya, and Somalia, and any other sanctioned or conflict-affected country globally.
How fast is settlement? +
USDT transfers settle same day, usually within 1–4 hours. BTC usually settles same day. Cash arrangements are completed within 24 hours in most locations. We prioritize speed for clients in urgent situations.
Can I buy XMR (Monero)? +
Yes. We support privacy coins for clients who need enhanced anonymity and financial privacy. Monero (XMR) is available for purchase and sale through our OTC desk. Contact us for current rates and availability.
