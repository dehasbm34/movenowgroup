Frequently Asked Questions

Can I send money to Iran, Sudan, or Afghanistan? +
Yes. We specialize in these corridors and have dedicated agents and networks in Iran, Sudan, and Afghanistan. These are among our most frequently served destinations.
Is this legal? +
We operate under applicable licenses. Personal remittances to most countries, including Iran, are generally permitted under OFAC general licenses (e.g., 31 C.F.R. § 560.550 for Iran). We advise all clients on their specific legal obligations before processing.
What is the maximum amount I can send? +
There is no hard limit. Larger amounts, typically above $10,000, may require additional documentation and identity verification. We handle high-value transfers routinely and will guide you through any requirements.
How does the recipient collect the money? +
It depends on the method chosen. For USDT transfers, the recipient receives funds directly to their crypto wallet or we convert to local currency via a local agent. For hawala, the recipient collects cash from a local agent using a transaction code. For bank wire, funds are deposited directly to their bank account.
What if my transfer fails? +
We provide a full refund for any undelivered transfers. Our success rate is extremely high, over 99%, but in the rare event of a failure, your funds are returned within 24 hours. No questions asked.
Do you report transfers to authorities? +
We comply with applicable reporting requirements under law. We inform clients in advance of any reporting obligations that may apply to their specific transaction. We do not share client data with any third party beyond what is legally required.
