Frequently Asked Questions

Documents & citizenship, answered plainly

How quickly can I get a second passport?

Vanuatu is fastest at roughly 30–60 days. Caribbean programs typically take 2–4 months plus due diligence. Türkiye 3–6 months. European golden visas 6–24 months. For emergency travel documents of your existing nationality (not citizenship), we can assist within 24–72 hours through official embassy and government urgent channels, where your issuing authority offers them.

I am Iranian, which programs can I access?

More than you may think. Vanuatu accepts applications from all nationalities. Türkiye is the most popular route for Iranians. Grenada, Antigua, and St. Lucia are open. Dominica requires 10+ years outside Iran. Jordan and Egypt accept Iranians. Malta explicitly excludes Iranian nationals. The UAE Golden Visa is widely used by Iranians, and Georgia is visa-free for 365 days. We give you a precise eligibility map in your free consultation, all subject to each government's due diligence.

Can I include my whole family in one application?

Yes, almost all CBI and residency programs include spouses and minor children at little or no extra cost. Most Caribbean programs also allow adult children up to 30, parents, and grandparents as dependents for additional fees. We structure family applications to maximize cost efficiency.

What is citizenship by descent and do I qualify?

If you have a grandparent or great-grandparent from Italy, Ireland, Poland, Hungary, Lithuania, Portugal, and several other countries, you may be entitled to citizenship, often for free. The process involves genealogy research, document collection, and a legal filing. We handle the entire process; book a free consultation and we can check your family history.

What is the difference between CBI and a golden visa?

CBI gives you full citizenship and a passport within months, no waiting period. A golden visa gives you the right to live in the country, with citizenship available after a qualifying residence period (typically 5–10 years). CBI is faster to a passport; a golden visa is usually lower cost but takes longer.

Do I have to move to or visit the country?

For Caribbean CBI and Vanuatu: no, zero physical presence required, ever. For European golden visas: minimal stays, Portugal about 7 days/year, Greece zero, UAE at least one visit every 2 years. We advise on exact requirements per program.

Will I pass due diligence?

Every reputable program runs multi-layer background checks covering identity, criminal history, sanctions screening, and source of funds. We pre-assess your file honestly before you commit, and we do not submit applications that would require misrepresentation, an application built on omissions can be revoked years later.
